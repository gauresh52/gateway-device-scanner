Gateway Device Scanner

Read printed device numbers with your camera or enter them manually.

The URL and token stay in this tab only; they are not saved by this page.

1. Read the printed device number

Allow camera access. Hold the printed serial label close, in focus, and well lit.

Hold the printed serial number close to the camera, keep it in focus, then click Read printed text. You can correct the result before adding it.

2. Confirm device number

Enter the Apps Script URL and token, then click Connect.

Duplicate IDs are blocked. Prefixes 1001 and 1027 are ambiguous in the current sheet, so select the exact model column for those devices.
